A dashboard-driven analysis of sales, profit, quantity, regional performance and product/category behavior, designed to make business performance easier to explore.

Sales performance

02

Profitability

Examined profit by category and sub-category, including negative-profit segments.

03

Customer segments

Used segment-level views to understand how Consumer, Corporate and Home Office contributed to sales.

03 — Key Findings

High sales and high profit are not always the same story.

  • West was the highest-sales region in the dashboard, followed by East.
  • Technology and Office Supplies were the strongest profit categories, while Furniture contributed much less profit.
  • At sub-category level, some products generated negative profit despite contributing sales, making profitability an important lens alongside revenue.
04 — Business Takeaway

Use revenue to understand scale, but use profit to understand quality of performance.

The dashboard brings both views together so product, category and regional decisions can be made with more context.
