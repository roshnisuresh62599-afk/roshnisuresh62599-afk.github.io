TableauPower BIEDABusiness Insights 01 — DASHBOARD PREVIEW Retail Sales Performance Tableau dashboardRetail Performance Power BI dashboard 02 — WHAT I ANALYZED 01 Sales performance Compared sales across regions and product/sub-category levels to identify the strongest contributors. 02 Profitability Examined profit by category and sub-category, including negative-profit segments. 03 Customer segments Used segment-level views to understand how Consumer, Corporate and Home Office contributed to sales. 03 — KEY FINDINGS High sales and high profit are not always the same story. West was the highest-sales region in the dashboard, followed by East. Technology and Office Supplies were the strongest profit categories, while Furniture contributed much less profit. At sub-category level, some products generated negative profit despite contributing sales, making profitability an important lens alongside revenue. 04 — BUSINESS TAKEAWAY Use revenue to understand scale, but use profit to understand quality of performance.
